CRITICAL FF-01 line 3

This field declares a constraint that rejects names outside the Basic Latin (ASCII) range.

Evidence
pattern="[A-Za-z]{2,40}" accepts "Smith" but rejects every tested name containing a letter outside U+0041–U+005A and U+0061–U+007A.
Remediation
Draw the permitted characters from the CLDR exemplar sets for the languages the form serves, or use a Unicode-aware class such as \p{L} with the punctuation names require.
<input id="fn" name="firstName" pattern="[A-Za-z]{2,40}" required>
HIGH FF-04 line 3

Legitimate name punctuation is rejected.

Evidence
pattern="[A-Za-z]{2,40}" rejects: apostrophe U+0027 — "O'Brien"; right single quotation mark U+2019 — "O’Brien"; hyphen-minus U+002D — "Anne-Marie"; space U+0020 — "van der Berg".
Remediation
Admit apostrophes, hyphens and internal spaces. Escaping on output is the defence against injection; input restriction is not.
<input id="fn" name="firstName" pattern="[A-Za-z]{2,40}" required>
HIGH FF-03 line 5

The constraint treats two identical-looking encodings of the same name differently.

Evidence
"Tāwhiao" accepted but its decomposed form rejected; "Émile" accepted but its decomposed form rejected; "Müller" accepted but its decomposed form rejected. The forms are canonically equivalent under UAX #15.
Remediation
Normalise the value before validating it, typically to NFC, so that canonically equivalent inputs are treated alike.
<input id="ln" name="lastName" pattern="[\p{L}\u0027\u2019 -]+">
MEDIUM FF-05 line 3

The field declares a minimum length that excludes short legitimate names.

Evidence
pattern="[A-Za-z]{2,40}" accepts nothing shorter than 2 characters.
Remediation
Allow a single character, or remove the lower bound from the pattern.
<input id="fn" name="firstName" pattern="[A-Za-z]{2,40}" required>
